# California Polytechnic State University (Cal Poly), San Luis Obispo's oSTEM x CPES (Computer Engineering Society) x Hackathon Spring 2026

## About:
- The Computer Engineering Society (**CPES**), a social and educational community for the Computer Engineering (as well as all computing) majors of California State Polytechnic University, San Luis Obispo! CPES is a student-run society whose primary goal is to help students succeed in their major and get a jump-start into the workforce.

- **oSTEM** chapter strives to create a welcoming community for queer engineers and scientists inside and outside of class. Through professional events, discussions between industry members and students, community spaces, outreach to local organizations and schools, and other forms of networking among the LGBTQ+ community, our oSTEM chapter will foster a more comfortable and inclusive space for queer people in STEM while focusing on the intersections of this community to ensure all members feel heard and empowered in STEM spaces at Cal Poly.

## Theme: Algorithmic Justice
**Challenge:** How can we redesign risk-assessment models to prevent identity-based discrimination in banking and insurance?

## Theme: Clinical Objectivity
**Challenge:** How can we build tools that separate a patient's gender-affirming care from unrelated medical diagnostics to ensure accurate and timely treatment?

## Theme: Integrity and Dignity
**Challenge:** How can we automate the complete erasure of "ghost data" across enterprise systems to ensure a person’s deadname never resurfaces after a legal change?

## Theme: Legacy Incompatibility
**Challenge:** How can we bridge the gap between modern inclusive identities and "hard-coded" legacy systems that only recognize binary genders or traditional marriage structures?

## Theme: Financial Interdependence
**Challenge:** How can we create "Shared Wallet" or joint-account tools for non-traditional families and "chosen family" structures that don't meet the narrow legal definition of a spouse?

## Theme: Safe Space Verification
**Challenge:** How can we build a reliable, real-time "Safety Score" for physical businesses to ensure LGBTQ+ people aren't walking into discriminatory environments?

## Previous Winner: MentalHealthMatters
**adda** was presented as an **AI-powered web app for mental wellness**, built around three connected experiences: **journaling, community, and AI-supported conversation**. Its core pitch was: *“AI-powered connection, journaling, and community for a healthier mind.”*

adda, an AI-powered mental wellness web app designed to support students through journaling, community, and meaningful connections. "adda" is the Bengali word for friendly gathering, with the goal of creating a safe, supportive space where students can feel heard and find belonging.

adda allows students to journal freely, with an AI that reads and responds to their thoughts and feelings in a conversational way. Based on their reflections, the AI suggests upcoming campus events, clubs, and activities that could help uplift their mood or align with their interests — bridging the gap between internal struggles and real-world support. 

adda also features an events tool, letting users discover upcoming events or create their own small-group gatherings — perfect for students who might feel overwhelmed in larger social settings. Whether it’s a game night, yoga session, or a substance-free hangout, adda empowers students to take charge of their own wellness journey. adda is inclusive for all social comfort levels and empower students to build the kind of community they need most.
