# Briefing Document: Clinical Objectivity and Contextual Privacy in Rehabilitation

## I. The Science of Objective Rehabilitation

The shift from subjective clinical observations to quantitative motion analysis is essential for identifying high-risk patients.

- **Gold Standard Metrics:** Force plate technology, such as the **AMTI** or **BTrackS** systems, measures fluctuations in the **Center of Pressure (CoP)** to quantify postural sway.

- **High-Reliability Indicators:** In sub-acute stroke recovery, **mean speed of AP-CoP** and **directional weight-bearing asymmetry (dir-WBA)** demonstrate the highest reliability, **ICC = 0.91**, for clinical decision-making.

- **Predictive Factors:** Beyond balance, the reduction in strength of knee flexors, knee extensors, and ankle dorsiflexors is significantly associated with balance impairment, with an **OR range of 0.82–0.88**.

## II. Advanced Cognitive-Motor Assessment

The project can implement complex protocols to evaluate functional ability in real-world scenarios.

- **Dual-Task Interference:** Stroke survivors often experience decreased gait speed and stability when performing cognitive tasks simultaneously.

- **Stroop Protocol:** Modern balance software includes the **Stroop Protocol**, which requires patients to manipulate their CoP to select response targets based on word meaning rather than color, testing executive function alongside postural control.

---

## III. Specialized Clinical Recovery & Precautions

For gender-affirming care, the project provides specific surgical context that is often absent in general rehab settings.

- **High Assistance Needs:** 47% of post-GAS patients require additional assistance in surgical recovery, yet many feel they must organize their own follow-up care due to institutional gaps.

- **Critical Movement Restrictions:**

  - **Vaginoplasty/Phalloplasty:** No sitting at 90 degrees upright, no sleeping on sides, and no excessive hip abduction for at least 12 weeks to protect surgical sites.

  - **Masculinizing Chest Surgery:** Avoid raising elbows above shoulders and heavy lifting, **greater than 5 lbs**, to prevent scar widening.

- **Specialized PT Roles:** 20% of post-GAS patients require **pelvic floor physiotherapy** to manage complications like vaginal stenosis or incontinence.

---

## IV. Privacy, Identity, and Justified Access

The project prioritizes a respectful clinical environment through intentional data management.

- **Identity Affirmation:** The platform preserves the **name in use and pronouns**, which are critical for reducing healthcare barriers for TGD, or transgender and gender-diverse, clients.

- **Justified Access:** By using visit context, such as a routine ankle visit, the platform protects unrelated sensitive history, including GAC, while revealing it only when necessary for safe post-op recovery.  
  **Source:** Project Summary

- **Audit Logging:** Continuous tracking of data access ensures accountability and patient trust in specialized care settings.
